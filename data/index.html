What we hold, and for how long.

Registering for a seminar, being recognised in the room, and receiving a certificate afterwards all involve a small amount of personal data. This page says what it is for and how to reach us about it; the full record it is drawn from is linked below, so this page never states a figure that record could later disagree with.

Access, correction, erasure

You can ask to see, correct, withdraw consent for, or erase what is held about you, before the retention deadline the record above sets out. Write to thebehaviourforum@gmail.com for any of this.
