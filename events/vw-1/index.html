01 Register

Before you register

We collect your first name, surname and email address to register you for this event and to issue your attendance certificate afterwards. Institution is optional. Nothing else is asked for.

Your browser encrypts this information before it is sent, using this event’s own key. Nobody — including us — can read it until an organiser decrypts it as part of running the event. The event’s data, encrypted form included, is permanently destroyed 90 days after the event by destroying the key that could ever read it again.

To access, correct or erase your data before that date, write to thebehaviourforum@gmail.com. Read the full data-protection record for what we hold, why, and for how long.
